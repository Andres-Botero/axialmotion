Billing

Who receives the payment

Effective September 22, 2026.

Axial Motion is a software studio. We operate Olyve — operations software for service businesses. Stripe charges for Olyve usage are received by Axial Motion. That is the name on the bank account and the name that appears on the customer’s card statement.

Customer billing, refunds, cancellation, terms, and support for those charges live on the product site:

getolyve.com/billing getolyve.com/refunds getolyve.com/terms getolyve.com/support

What Olyve is

Digital software: clients, visits, estimates, invoices, and inbox. Prices are in United States dollars (USD) and are shown before payment. Access is delivered online. There is no physical shipping.

Web payments are processed by Stripe. Axial Motion does not store full card numbers. App Store and Google Play purchases are billed by Apple or Google.

Other studio products

We also build Linkflow and Today. Those products are not what this Stripe account is charging for.
