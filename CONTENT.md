- Readers are company owners and operators, not investors.

## What the studio is

Axial Motion builds tools and automation for company owners and operators —
software that shortens a real job (a visit, a referral, a day of work).

Products to name, in this order:

1. **Olyve** — operations for service businesses. [getolyve.com](https://getolyve.com)
2. **Linkflow** — referral programs. [linkflow.link](https://linkflow.link)
3. **Today** — three goals for the day. [today.andybotero.com](https://today.andybotero.com)

Do not invent a fourth product. Do not hide any of the three.
